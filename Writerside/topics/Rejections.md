# Review &amp; Rejections

Following submission by a Data Capturer, the designated reviewer evaluates the submission to either approve or reject it.

Any rejection routes the submission back to the preceding responsibility level for necessary revisions and resubmission.

## Review Hierarchy and Workflow

The review process follows a defined hierarchy: **Data Capturer** → **Programme Manager** → **Corporate Planning**.

Both Programme Managers and Corporate Planning reviewers have the authority to reject a submission when reported data or supporting documentation does not meet required standards.

> - Submissions rejected by **Corporate Planning** are returned to the **Programme Manager** for revision.
> - Submissions rejected by the **Programme Manager** are returned to the **Data Capturer** for revision.
{style="info"}

## Rejection Justification

When rejecting a Programme Overview or an indicator submission, the reviewer is required to provide a clear, formal explanation detailing the reasons for rejection and the corrective actions required.
