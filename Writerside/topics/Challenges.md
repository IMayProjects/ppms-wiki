# Challenges

The **Challenges** tab provides a structured narrative section to document operational obstacles encountered during the quarter, assess their impact on indicator delivery, and specify remedial interventions.

![capture-challenges.png](capture-challenges.png){width="720"}

The following fields are mandatory:

1. <shortcut>Challenges Encountered</shortcut>: Describe any operational, financial, or administrative difficulties experienced during the reporting period.
2. <shortcut>Observed Impact</shortcut>: Detail how these challenges impacted indicator performance and planned milestone delivery.
3. <shortcut>Corrective Measures</shortcut>: Specify the mitigation strategies and corrective actions implemented or planned to address the challenges.

Once completed, click [Next: POE & Submission](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"} to proceed to evidence submission.
