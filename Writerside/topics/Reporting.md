# Reporting

During the quarterly reporting cycle, the Data Capturer submits actual performance achievements against the approved annual targets. In addition to performance data, the reporting module allows capturers to record any budget adjustments that occurred during the quarter.

## Quarterly Reporting Workflow

The quarterly submission process consists of four consecutive stages:

1. [Budget](Budget.md): Confirm whether the indicator's budget allocation has changed, and record any approved revisions.
2. [Target Achievement](Target-Achievement.md): Record actual performance values, quarter-to-date expenditure, key activities executed, and reasons for any variance.
3. [Challenges](Challenges.md): Document operational challenges encountered during the quarter, assess their impact, and outline corrective actions.
4. [Evidence & Submission](Evidence-Submission.md): Upload supporting Portfolio of Evidence (POE) documentation and submit the quarterly report for review.
