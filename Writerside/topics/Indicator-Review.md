# Review an Indicator

![](pm-review-actions.png)

To begin reviewing an indicator, click on the **_eye_** ![](fa-eye-512.png){width="24"} icon, under the `Actions` column.

![pm-review-popup.png](pm-review-popup.png)

A pop-up window will appear showing you the captured submission as well as the technical indicator description.

After reviewing the submission data you may either click [Accept](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"} or [Reject](0:0){as="button" mode="classic" nullable="true"}

If the submission is rejected by the `Programme Manager`, it will return to the `Data Capturer` for corrections.
