# Target Achievement

On the `Actuals` tab, you will submit the actual achievement data for the indicator

![actuals.png](actuals.png){width="800"}

Below the heading "Actual Performance" you will see the targets from quarter 1 to 4.

Below the target data, the following fields must be filled:

1. <shortcut>Quarter Achievement (Actual)</shortcut> The actual achievement value
2. <shortcut>Budget Spent This Quarter</shortcut> The amount of budget spent during the quarter, towards the achievement of the target. 
3. <shortcut>Key Activities Achieved</shortcut>
4. <shortcut>Reason for Deviation</shortcut>

When done, you may proceed by clicking [Next: Challenges](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"}
