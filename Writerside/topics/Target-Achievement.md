# Target Achievement

On the **Actuals** tab, record the verified quarterly performance data for the selected indicator.

![actuals.png](actuals.png){width="720"}

The **Actual Performance** section displays the planned milestone targets for Quarters 1 through 4.

Below the planned target figures, complete the following mandatory fields:

1. <shortcut>Quarter Achievement (Actual)</shortcut>: Enter the verified achievement value realized during the quarter.
2. <shortcut>Budget Spent This Quarter</shortcut>: Enter the expenditure incurred during the quarter toward target delivery in Rands (ZAR).
3. <shortcut>Key Activities Achieved</shortcut>: Summarize the key operational activities successfully completed during the quarter.
4. <shortcut>Reason for Deviation</shortcut>: Provide a formal justification explaining any variance between the planned target and the actual achievement.

Once completed, click [Next: Challenges](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"} to proceed to the challenges narrative.
