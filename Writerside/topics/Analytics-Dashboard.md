# Analytics Dashboard

The Analytics Dashboard provides departmental leadership, Programme Managers, and oversight officials with consolidated visibility into performance metrics, target achievement, and budget expenditure.

## Overview

The dashboard visualizes key performance indicators (KPIs) across all active programmes in the Eastern Cape Department of Education. It enables stakeholders to monitor quarterly progress, identify variances, and support evidence-based strategic decision-making.

Key capabilities include:

- **Performance Tracking**: Monitor quarterly target achievement rates across all outcome indicators.
- **Financial Monitoring**: Track budget allocations against actual quarterly expenditure.
- **Variance Analysis**: Identify indicators experiencing negative or positive deviations from planned targets.
- **Executive Reporting**: Generate consolidated analytics summaries for audit and oversight reviews.
