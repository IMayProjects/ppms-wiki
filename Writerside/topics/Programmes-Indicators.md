# Programmes &amp; Indicators

The Programmes and Indicators configuration module allows System Administrators to establish and maintain the performance monitoring framework for the Eastern Cape Department of Education.

## Framework Configuration

Administrators configure the performance architecture according to the Annual Performance Plan (APP) and Strategic Plan:

1. **Programme Configuration**: Define departmental programmes and sub-programmes, specifying programme leadership and oversight assignments.
2. **Indicator Setup**: Register custom and standardized outcome indicators, assigning unique identifier codes and performance categories.
3. **Technical Indicator Descriptions (TIDs)**: Define technical measurement standards, calculation methodologies, data sources, frequency of reporting, and required Portfolio of Evidence (POE) specifications for each indicator.
4. **Programme Mapping**: Associate indicators with their designated programmes to make them available to Data Capturers and Programme Managers during planning and reporting cycles.
