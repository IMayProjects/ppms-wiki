# Evidence &amp; Submission

Upon reaching the `Evidence & Submission` tab you will see a summary of the submission.

Below the summary, upload all relevant supporting evidence.

![capture-poe-submit.png](../images/capture-poe-submit.png){width="720"}

When done, you may proceed by clicking [Submit for Review](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"}

after clicking, a confirmation prompt will appear:

![capture-submit-confirm.png](../images/capture-submit-confirm.png){width="320"}

Click [OK](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"} to confirm or  [Cancel](0:0){as="button" mode="outline" nullable="true"} to go back.
