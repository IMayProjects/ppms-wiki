# Evidence &amp; Submission

The **Evidence & Submission** tab presents a consolidated summary of all data captured across the preceding reporting tabs.

Below the summary section, upload all relevant Portfolio of Evidence (POE) files that verify and substantiate the reported achievement.

![capture-poe-submit.png](../images/capture-poe-submit.png){width="720"}

Once all documentation has been uploaded, click [Submit for Review](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"}.

A confirmation prompt will appear:

![capture-submit-confirm.png](../images/capture-submit-confirm.png){width="320"}

Click [OK](0:0){as="button" nullable="true"} to confirm and submit the report, or click [Cancel](0:0){as="button" mode="outline" nullable="true"} to return to the form and make further adjustments.
