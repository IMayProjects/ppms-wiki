# Reporting Periods

The Reporting Periods module enables System Administrators to establish financial years, configure reporting timelines, and regulate submission deadlines for annual planning and quarterly reporting.

## Configure Planning and Reporting Periods

System Administrators govern system availability through the following configurations:

1. **Financial Year Management**: Define the active financial year and set it as the operational default across the system.
2. **Submission Deadlines**: Establish hard deadlines for annual planning submissions and quarterly reporting cycles (Quarters 1 through 4).
3. **Module Locking**: If a submission deadline has not yet passed, data capture and editing remain accessible to users. Once the deadline expires, the corresponding reporting window is locked automatically to prevent unauthorized modifications.
